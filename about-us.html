About us

A structural engineering firm built around the coast

Founded in 2011 on Oahu's North Shore, Allshore Engineering handles projects of every size, from a single residential retaining wall to harbor-wide dock replacements.

Who we are

Small firm. Broad experience.

Allshore Engineering, LLC is a full-service structural engineering firm. We design, evaluate, inspect and repair structures of all types, sizes and levels of complexity. Our roots are in marine and coastal work, and that experience carries over to buildings and infrastructure inland.

We keep the firm lean on purpose. Clients get direct access to the principal engineer. For larger or specialized work, we bring in a trusted network of geotechnical, civil, marine and architectural professionals. The result is timely, cost-efficient projects in Hawaii, on the mainland and overseas.

Principal

Joshua M. Murphree, P.E., S.E.

Structural Engineer & President

Josh has more than 20 years of experience in engineering and construction. His work spans coastal and marine structures, infrastructure, commercial and residential buildings, commercial diving, marine construction project management, land development, civil design, and building-code and hazard-modeling research.

As a certified commercial diver, he inspects waterfront structures from below the waterline. As a former marine construction project manager, he designs with the builder's means and methods in mind.

How we work

A straightforward process

Listen & scope

We review your goals, site and constraints, then send a clear proposal with defined scope and fee.

Investigate

We do site visits, condition surveys, underwater inspection and testing where needed to understand what's really there.

Engineer

Our calculations and construction drawings meet current codes and Hawaii's wind, seismic and marine demands.

Support construction

We handle permit review responses, RFIs, submittals and observation through completion.

Have a project in mind?

Tell us what you're building, repairing or evaluating. We'll follow up with next steps and a clear scope.
