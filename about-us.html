About Allshore Engineering

A structural engineering firm based in Haleiwa, Oahu, providing design services throughout Hawaii since 2011.

The firm

Allshore Engineering, LLC is a structural engineering firm founded in 2011. The firm has completed more than 900 projects, ranging from single-family homes and additions to commercial buildings, marine structures and one-off specialty structures.

Most of our work is new design: structural calculations, construction drawings and permit support, followed by construction-phase services such as submittal review, responses to RFIs and observation. We also perform condition assessments and inspections.

For larger or specialized projects we work alongside geotechnical, civil and architectural consultants.

Principal

Joshua M. Murphree, P.E., S.E.

Structural Engineer, President

Josh has more than 20 years of experience in structural engineering and construction. His design work covers residential and commercial buildings, specialty and industrial structures, marine and coastal structures, and infrastructure. Earlier in his career he worked in marine construction project management, land development and civil design, and in building code and hazard modeling research.

He is also a certified commercial diver, which allows underwater inspection of waterfront structures when a project requires it.

Process

Project process

Proposal

We review the architectural drawings, site and schedule, then provide a written proposal with scope and fee.

Design

Structural analysis and calculations under the current building code, including Hawaii wind, seismic and flood requirements.

Documents & permit

Stamped structural drawings and calculations, and responses to plan review comments.

Construction

Submittal review, RFI responses, structural observation and special inspection as required.

Discuss a project

Send the project location, a short description and any drawings you have. We will reply with a proposed scope and fee.
