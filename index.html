Services

Structural engineering services

Our work covers custom homes and renovations, commercial buildings, docks, piers and other waterfront structures, bridges and civil structures, and a wide range of unusual structures that fall outside standard building types.

Residential

Custom homes, additions, renovations, ADUs, garages, decks and retaining walls.

Commercial & Institutional

New buildings, renovations, metal buildings, churches, schools and hospitality projects.

Specialty Structures

Equipment foundations, lifting and test frames, canopies, shade structures, solar racking and towers.

Marine & Waterfront

Marinas, floating docks, gangways, piers, seawalls, moorings and harbor structures.

Assessments & Inspections

Condition and damage assessments, pre-purchase evaluations, load ratings and special inspections.

Infrastructure

Bridge evaluation and load rating, culverts, retaining walls and utility structures.
Approach

How we work

  • Each project is designed and stamped by a licensed structural engineer who stays involved from proposal through construction.
  • Designs account for Hawaii’s wind, seismic, flood and coastal requirements, and for county plan review.
  • We work with architects, designers, contractors and owners, and coordinate with geotechnical and civil consultants as needed.
  • We take on complex and unusual structures, including one-off equipment supports and temporary works.
