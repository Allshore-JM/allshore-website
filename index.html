What we do

Engineering from the seafloor up

Why Allshore

Hands-on engineering, start to finish

You work directly with a licensed structural engineer from start to finish. The engineer who stamps your drawings is the same one who walks your site and, when needed, dives your structure.

  • Island conditions, built in. High wind and seismic demands, corrosive marine exposure and Honolulu DPP plan review are routine for us.
  • Built to be built. We work closely with marine contractors, so our designs are practical to fabricate and install.
  • Above and below the waterline. Certified diving lets us inspect what most engineers can only estimate.
  • Right-sized team. We bring in trusted specialists as needed to keep projects on schedule and on budget.
