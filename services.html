Services

Services

Structural design, construction documents and construction-phase services, along with assessments and inspections.

Residential

Homes, additions and renovations

Residential design makes up most of our work. We provide structural design for custom homes, production and model homes, additions, renovations and accessory structures, working from the architect’s or designer’s drawings.

  • Custom homes
  • Additions and second stories
  • Renovations and remodels
  • ADUs and ohana units
  • Garages, carports and lanais
  • Retaining walls
  • Coastal and flood-zone homes
  • Pole, post-and-pier and hillside foundations
Commercial & Institutional

Commercial buildings

Structural design for new commercial buildings, tenant improvements and renovations, along with evaluation of existing buildings.

  • New buildings
  • Pre-engineered metal buildings
  • Churches and schools
  • Hotel and resort projects
  • Renovations and tenant improvements
  • Mezzanines, roof decks and canopies
  • Equipment anchorage for wind and seismic loads
  • Agricultural structures
Specialty Structures

Complex and one-off structures

We design structures that do not fit a standard building type, often with short schedules and close coordination with fabricators and contractors.

  • Machine and equipment foundations
  • Lifting frames, spreader bars and lift plans
  • Load-test frames and fixtures
  • Crane and gantry supports
  • Shade structures and canopies
  • Solar racking and supports
  • Towers and poles
  • Temporary works and shoring
Marine & Waterfront

Waterfront structures

Design and evaluation of structures at harbors and along the shoreline, often as the design engineer for marine contractors.

  • Floating docks and gangways
  • Piers and wharves
  • Seawalls
  • Bollards and fendering
  • Moorings
Assessments & Inspections

Assessments and inspections

Evaluation of existing structures for owners, buyers, contractors and agencies. Clients with active projects can use the Allshore Inspection Program to submit construction observation and special inspection checklists.

  • Condition assessments
  • Pre-purchase structural evaluations
  • Storm, fire and earthquake damage evaluations
  • Special inspection and structural observation
  • Load ratings
  • Underwater inspection
Infrastructure

Bridges and civil structures

Evaluation and design of bridges and other civil structures.

  • Bridge evaluation and load rating
  • Culverts
  • Retaining walls
  • Utility structures
