Services

Design, evaluation and inspection

We provide structural engineering for new construction, repairs and assessments across four core markets.

Marine & Waterfront

Marinas, docks and harbor structures

Our specialty. We design and evaluate the structures that live in the harshest environment there is, with specialized expertise in aluminum and in delegated design for marine contractors.

  • Marina and small boat harbor design
  • Aluminum floating docks and gangways
  • Pier and wharf repair and evaluation
  • Mooring evaluation and design
  • Offshore and nearshore structures
  • Bollards, cleats and fender systems
  • Underwater structural inspection
Infrastructure

Bridges, walls and civil structures

We inspect, load-rate and design the infrastructure Hawaii's communities depend on, including state and county bridges and private crossings on rural lots.

  • Bridge inspection and load rating
  • Bridge repair and new design
  • Pre-purchase bridge assessments
  • Retaining walls and stabilization
  • Micropile and underpinning design
  • Utility and miscellaneous structures
Commercial

Buildings, assessments and special inspections

Structural design for new buildings and renovations, plus assessments that tell owners exactly what they have.

  • New building design
  • Renovations and repairs
  • Forensic evaluations
  • Condition assessments
  • Special inspections
  • Seismic and wind anchorage of equipment
  • ADA ramps and accessibility structures
  • Agricultural structures
Residential

Homes, additions and inspections

Practical structural engineering for homeowners, architects and builders, with designs that pass plan review and hold up to island wind and weather.

  • New home design
  • Additions and remodels
  • Structural repairs
  • Pre-purchase structural inspections
  • Retaining walls
  • Plan review comment responses
